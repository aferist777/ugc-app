# Recipes

Whole paths from an idea to a rendered video, a step a line. Each step leads to the chapter that tells it in full.

## An ad: a talking head with your product

1. **The person** - Library → Characters → **New Character**, or one you have ([Characters](03-the-library.md#characters)).
2. **The product** - IMAGES → Props → **create**: its photo, its name, a few words ([New prop](08-generating.md#environments-lookbooks-and-props)).
3. **The place** - IMAGES → Environments → **create** from a photo and a sentence, or one made before.
4. **The script** - Library → Scenarios → **New scenario**: write it, a line a paragraph, the hook first - or let the
   narrator generator write it ([Scenarios](03-the-library.md#scenarios)). **Make video** opens it in Merge.
5. **Who and where** - the look and the environment ([Who and where](05-talking-head.md#who-and-where)).
6. **Mood & voice** → **generate**: every line is read.
7. **Frames chat** - ask for the frames: "close-up, she holds the pump up to the camera"; put the product in
   **Product · from Props**; **to frames** puts a frame into the set ([Frames](05-talking-head.md#2-frames)).
8. **Video** → **generate**, then **… · go?** - a clip for every line ([Video](05-talking-head.md#3-video)).
9. On the timeline: **Captions** → a preset - the lines' words are heard by themselves ([Captions](04-merge.md#captions)).
   Add B-roll and music if you like (the recipes below), then **Export** → **Render**.

## A voiceover over stock footage

1. **Home → Voiceover**: a scenario or your own text, the mood, the voice → **create** ([Starting one](06-voiceover.md#starting-one)).
2. **generate** - the script is read.
3. **find B-roll** - footage for every sentence, laid over the voice ([B-roll](06-voiceover.md#2-b-roll)).
4. Press a piece that does not fit and pick another from what was found ([Swapping a piece](06-voiceover.md#swapping-a-piece)).
5. **Captions** → a preset - the words were heard already, for the B-roll.
6. **Export** → **Render**.

## Shorts from a podcast or an interview

1. **Home → Highlights**: the video - a link, the Library, or a file ([Step 1](07-highlights.md#step-1-the-video-and-the-fragments)).
2. **Podcast · interview**, the length (30-60 s is a good start), how many → **Next →**.
3. Music moods and a caption preset → **Find moments** ([Step 2](07-highlights.md#step-2-music-and-captions)).
4. When the tiles stand: press each to look at it; delete the weak ones (✕), rename the good ones.
5. **Select all** → change what is common with the four **· for all N** buttons ([Several fragments at once](07-highlights.md#several-fragments-at-once)).
6. **Render N** - every short in the project's renders folder.

## A B-roll clip from a picture

1. **IMAGES → generate** - the pictures chat: describe the picture, the shape 9:16 ([The pictures chat](08-generating.md#the-pictures-chat)).
2. The right button on the picture made (in the Library, or on the timeline) → **Animate** - the video chat opens with it
   as the first frame.
3. A cheap model for a try - **Seedance 2.0 mini** or **Grok** -, a sentence of what moves → send; with the writer on,
   look at its prompt and press **Make the video** ([The video chat](08-generating.md#the-video-chat)).
4. Drag the clip onto a video lane above main video - an insert over the speaker.

## Music made for a video

1. On the timeline, the right button on a clip → **Video to Music…** - the sound window opens with that clip.
2. Say the mood if you like → **Generate**: the track is laid on a sound lane of its own, under the clip
   ([The sound window](08-generating.md#the-sound-window)).
3. Or **AUDIO → generate** → **Simple** - background music with no voice, its length "by the video".
4. Turn the music down on its lane so the voice stays on top.

## Your own recording: pauses out, captions in

1. **+ New project → blank**; drag your video file from Explorer onto main video - it is copied into VIDEO → Uploads.
2. Sweep over the whole wave, then **S** - **Cut silence** shortens every pause at once ([Cut silence](04-merge.md#cut-silence)).
3. The Subtitles lane → **Generate from the sound**; correct a word in **Captions → Text** if it was misheard.
4. **Captions** → a preset; drag the captions on the preview where they should stand.
5. **Export** → **Render**.

## A piece of a long video

1. Library → Media → **From a link** (or **Upload**) - the video lands in the open folder.
2. Open it: sweep over the bar under it to draw fragments, or **Scenes** to find the shots ([The viewer](03-the-library.md#the-viewer-and-cutting-a-video)).
3. **▶** plays them in a row; **Save…** joins them into one new clip next to the video.
4. Drag the clip onto the timeline.

## From your phone

1. Send your Telegram bot a link to a post, a photo or a video ([REFERENCES](03-the-library.md#references-and-the-telegram-bot)).
2. It arrives in **REFERENCES** - a tile a post. Open it; cut a piece as above, or attach its pictures in a chat as
   references.
