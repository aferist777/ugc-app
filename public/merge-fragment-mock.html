Where the result goes
Replacing swaps the picture and keeps the audio that is already on the clip — a generated video brings its own silence, and dropping the read with it is how a fragment goes quiet without anyone noticing.
Runs collect here. Only the ticked ones are saved — nothing is filed until you close this.

A still fragment

Double-clicking a picture opens Animate, which is already built and already does this — a picture, a sentence about how it should move, a length. Nothing new is needed here beyond wiring the double-click to it; today it is reached by a button in the inspector.

Which means the two gestures answer the same question in the same place: double-click asks “make me something from this”, and what opens depends only on whether the clip is a picture or a video.
