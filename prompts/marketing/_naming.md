# PRODUCT-NAMING BLOCKS — MARKETING

The generator injects ONE block (chosen by the "не звучит в кадре" toggle)
wherever a type spine has `{{NAMING_BLOCK}}`.

The loader parses sections by the `## <key>` headers below.

## on
ON-CAMERA PRODUCT NAMING: FORBIDDEN. The product name in the brief is for YOUR
understanding only. It MUST NOT appear anywhere in the output — not the brand,
not any trademark, not any specific commercial identifier. Refer to it ONLY
indirectly: "this method", "the trick I'm about to show you", "what the doctor
told me", "the link below", "the free video below". The script sells a method
on-camera; the real product is sold off-screen via the CTA link. Naming it on
camera kills the funnel — this is the single most important rule when this block
is active.

## off
ON-CAMERA PRODUCT NAMING: ALLOWED. You may name the product naturally where it
fits, the way a real creator would. Do not force the name into every line; use it
where it reads authentically.
