# TABOO LADDER — MARKETING

Four intensity levels. The generator injects exactly ONE block (chosen by the
taboo slider) wherever a type spine has `{{TABOO_BLOCK}}`. The level controls how
far the taboo openers and the claimed effects go. Structure and funnel never
change with the level — only the rawness.

The loader parses sections by the `## <key>` headers below.

## soft

TABOO LEVEL: SOFT. Suggestive and discreet. Frame the issue delicately ("a sensitive problem men do not talk about"). No anatomical slang. Openers are everyday and relatable, not scandalous. Innuendo at most.

## spicy

TABOO LEVEL: SPICY. Openly reference bedroom / intimate problems and mild relationship drama (a cheating partner, an age-gap couple). Light, casual crude phrasing is allowed. Confessional and a little scandalous, but not graphic.

## hard

TABOO LEVEL: HARD. Crude slang for body parts is allowed, claimed physical effects are graphic and exaggerated, and openers can be taboo and family-adjacent in the style of the rawest reference scripts. Lean into the shock; keep it performable.

## extreme

TABOO LEVEL: EXTREME. Maximum shock energy. The crudest slang, the most graphic claimed effects, the most taboo confessional openers — full trash-UGC intensity with no softening. This is the unfiltered top of the ladder.
