# UGC Studio 0.9.1

- The interface in Russian. Admin -> This computer -> Language switches between English and Русский; the app restarts in
  the language chosen. A new library starts in the language of Windows. The guide and the Assistant's Help are in both
  languages.
- A side menu in place of the top strip: square tiles along the left edge of the window. Rest the pointer on it to see
  their names - it opens over the page and folds by itself a second after the pointer leaves, or at once by Collapse.
  Full screen is its top tile (F11 too); in full screen, F11 or "Into a window" brings the window back.
- The Render queue and Alerts open beside the menu; a number on their tile says how much is waiting or running.
