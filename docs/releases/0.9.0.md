# UGC Studio 0.9.0

The first public version: usable, still in development. Windows only.

- Characters with their looks, environments, lookbooks and products - made and kept in one library.
- Scripts in a narrator's style; talking heads and voiceovers made from them line by line, with stock B-roll.
- Merge: a timeline with captions, effects, patterns, colour, pitch and cut silence.
- Highlights: a long video into vertical shorts whose frame follows the faces or the speaker.
- References from YouTube, Instagram and TikTok saved through your own Telegram bot.
- You pay only for what you generate, with your own keys, straight to the services.

Unzip it, open "UGC Studio.exe" and enter your keys in Admin -> API keys. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC":
the program is not signed - press "More info", then "Run anyway". Updates come by themselves.
